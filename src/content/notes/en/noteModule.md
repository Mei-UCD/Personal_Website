## Why I Added Notes

This website originally started as a place for me to organize my projects and work. But as I kept building it, I realized there were still a lot of things I wanted to write down, without really knowing where they belonged.

Things that suddenly came to mind, a record from a trip, something I learned the hard way while working on a project, something I've been tinkering with lately, or simply a thought I had on a particular day.

They don't really belong in a Project, so I created 「Notes」.

There won't be a fixed theme here, and not every note needs to be complete. It might be about technology, life, or simply something I'm thinking about at the moment.

It's just a little corner where I can write whatever comes to mind.
