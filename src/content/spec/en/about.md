## About This Site

This website is developed based on the [Gyoza template](https://www.lxchapu.com) and is used to showcase my personal projects and learning outcomes.

The main content includes:

- **Design & Architecture**: BIM modeling, parametric façade design, and related architectural projects
- **Programming & Technology**: Web development, image processing, and AI project practices
- **Personal Notes & Reflections**: Design theory, learning notes, and practical tutorials

The purpose of this website is not only to **document my work and learning journey**, but also to **connect with people who share similar interests**.

## About Me

My name is **Mei**. I used to work as an architectural designer, and I am working as an image processing engineer now. I also have a strong curiosity about **software/Web development and artificial intelligence**.

From time to time, I record my learning process and side projects here—almost like keeping a diary. I hope that one day, when I look back, I can still feel the thoughts and inspirations I had at that moment.
