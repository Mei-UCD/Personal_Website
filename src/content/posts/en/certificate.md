Luckily, I finally had some free time these days, so I decided to record my experience taking Japan's Fundamental Information Technology Engineer Examination.

## About FE

The 「基本情報技術者試験」 (Fundamental Information Technology Engineer Examination, abbreviated as FE) is a national-level IT certification exam organized by the Information-technology Promotion Agency, Japan (IPA).

The exam consists of two sections: Subject A and Subject B. Both are computer-based multiple-choice exams. Subject A has 60 questions with a 90-minute time limit, while Subject B has 20 questions with a 100-minute time limit. There is also an optional 10-minute break in between.

The overall passing rate is around 40%.

I did a little research before taking the exam. Although this certification covers relatively fundamental IT knowledge, it seems to be quite recognized in Japan. There are also many examples of people from non-IT backgrounds successfully transitioning into tech with this certification. Most importantly, it can add 5 points to the Highly Skilled Professional visa points system (★ ω ★).

## Preparation

I registered for the exam on the [IPA official website](https://www.ipa.go.jp/shiken/kubun/fe.html) around early May and scheduled the exam for early June. The exam fee was 7,500 yen. It is not extremely expensive, but this amount of money can still buy me three meals of mala xiang guo (drooling)...

Of course, if you pass on the first try, it is fine. But taking the exam multiple times can become quite expensive.

Originally, I planned to use the 10-day Golden Week holiday in Japan to prepare seriously. However, I ended up spending the entire holiday looking for apartments and moving-related stuff. Apparently, when you need to study, everything else suddenly becomes much more interesting 😅.

After the holiday ended, I realized there were only about four weeks left. I was anxious mentally, but my body and brain did not really cooperate. Plus, I still had to work and sometimes work overtime on weekdays, so my study time from Monday to Friday was basically zero.

Therefore, I gave up buying textbooks and started by checking the exam structure on [Fundamental Information Technology Engineer Past Questions Dojo](https://www.fe-siken.com/).

The exam covers three major fields:

- テクノロジ系 (Technology)
- マネジメント系 (Management)
- ストラテジ系 (Strategy)

Each major field is further divided into many smaller topics. You can check the details on the Past Questions Dojo website.

For Subject A, I mainly followed the FE-related courses by YouTuber [すーさん](https://www.youtube.com/@kihonzyouhou/courses). I watched the videos according to the FE exam categories and took notes while learning.

After finishing each topic, I solved 5 questions from the corresponding category on the Past Questions Dojo.

Yes, only 5 questions 😂. I wanted to do more, but my time and energy had limits.

The total study time was roughly around 15 hours.

For Subject B, I only looked at one year of sample questions on the Past Questions Dojo and finished my preparation there. Then I went to the exam in a hurry 😂.

## Exam Experience

Since the exam is entirely multiple-choice, I felt the overall difficulty was not too high.

Subject A covers topics such as computer hardware, storage, information security, networking, and more. Subject B mainly focuses on algorithms and information security.

Personally, I found Subject A harder than Subject B.

If you have programming experience and have written code before, understanding how programs work makes Subject B much easier. I think you can probably pass it with little preparation after just checking some past questions.

For Subject A, maybe because I also came from a career transition into tech and my computer science fundamentals were not very solid, I found the wide range of topics quite challenging. Especially networking and information security felt difficult.

During Subject A, I honestly felt like I was choosing many answers based on intuition. Before submitting, I thought there was a good chance I might fail.

When I clicked the submit button, I was pretty nervous because the result appears immediately after submitting the exam.

Luckily, I passed both Subject A and Subject B, although just barely 😂.

The feeling of getting a high return from low investment was amazing!

I immediately went shopping and rewarded myself with a bunch of things.

## A Side Note

Since I passed FE with very little preparation, I thought I could take advantage of the momentum and immediately challenge the Information Security Management Examination (SG).

Because FE also covers information security topics and there are some overlapping areas, I registered for the SG exam two weeks later.

Well... apparently I was too confident.

Information security was only a relatively small part of FE, so I was able to survive with some luck. But in SG, information security is basically the main focus.

So I failed 😭.

I still have not fully recovered from this. I will write about it in detail later...
