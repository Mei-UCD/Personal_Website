### 🧭 Project Background

**Donghu Technology City** is located in the eastern new district of Dongtai City, Jiangsu Province. It is a provincial-level key service-industry project, planned as an ecological coastal technology innovation city integrating technology, finance, commerce, and residential functions.

This project focuses on the **curtain wall engineering of the hotel complex building in Phase I of Donghu Technology City**. My primary responsibility was the **BIM modeling of the building façade curtain wall system and the generation of design drawings during the tender stage**.

---

### 🧱 Project Information

- **Project Name**: Curtain Wall Engineering of the Phase I Hotel Complex, Donghu Technology City
- **Project Positioning**: Landmark curtain wall system design and detailing for a core technology innovation district
- **Project Scale**: Total gross floor area of the hotel complex is approximately **383,615.16 ㎡**
- **Location**: Donghu Technology City Phase I, Eastern New District, Dongtai City, Jiangsu Province
- **Project Type**: Provincial-level key service-industry project

### 🧩 BIM Modeling and Design Workflow

This project primarily utilized **Revit** and **Rhinoceros** as the main tools for modeling and coordination, completing the curtain wall system modeling and panel layout design.

As the project was in the **tender stage**, the BIM model focused on **visual presentation and design expression**, rather than construction-level detailing.

The workflow mainly included the following stages:

#### 1. **Concept Model Development**

Based on architectural elevations, plans, and grid systems, preliminary curtain wall system models were created using Revit and Rhino.

### 🔮 Summary and Reflection

- **Responsibilities**: Responsible for the BIM modeling and coordination of the hotel complex curtain wall system. Completed curtain wall panel layout, panel numbering, and drawing output using Revit and Rhino. Also participated in the production of presentation models to enhance bidding competitiveness.
- **Challenges**: As the model was intended for presentation rather than construction, a balance had to be achieved between modeling accuracy and efficiency.
- **Solutions**: Rapid mass modeling and curtain wall layout were achieved using Revit massing tools, enabling efficient production of complete elevation drawings.
- **Key Takeaways**: This project deepened my understanding of the differences between presentation-level BIM models and construction-level models. It also strengthened my experience in rapid modeling and visualization during the tender stage, while reinforcing integrated workflows between Rhino and Revit for complex curtain wall projects.
