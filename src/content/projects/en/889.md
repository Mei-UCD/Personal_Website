### 🧭 Project Background

**Yancheng Yueda 889 Urban Plaza** is located at the intersection of Hope Avenue and Qingnian Road Elevated Road in Tinghu District, Yancheng City, Jiangsu Province (east of the Tinghu District Government). It is a comprehensive urban commercial landmark integrating **shopping, lifestyle, entertainment, family-oriented activities, and social interaction**.

The project has a total gross floor area of approximately **130,000 m²**, including a **90,000 m² shopping mall** and **40,000 m² of hotel and office spaces**.  
As one of Yancheng’s key urban image projects, the development aims to create a multifunctional “urban living room” that combines commercial vitality with human-centered spatial experiences, offering a full-spectrum lifestyle destination for city residents.

### 🧩 BIM Modeling Workflow

#### 1. **Definition of Modeling Objectives and Scope**

- **Modeling scope**: Curtain wall façade systems (glass curtain wall, stone curtain wall, stone-textured aluminum panels, aluminum panels, and real stone paint), as well as primary structural components (beams, columns, and slabs)
- **BIM tool**: Rhinoceros
- **Clash detection scope**: Curtain wall systems vs. structural beams and columns
- **Deliverables**: Curtain wall design and construction drawings, including elevations, plans, and detailed sections

### 🔮 Summary & Reflection

- **Responsibilities**: Rhino modeling, curtain wall design and construction drawings (elevations, plans, and detailed drawings)
- **Challenges**:
  - Multiple curtain wall systems (glass, stone, stone-textured aluminum panels, and aluminum panels), resulting in complex node design
  - Complex positioning of irregular façade geometries, increasing design and construction difficulty
- **Solutions**: Precise Rhino modeling significantly reduced design conflicts and improved construction accuracy
- **Key Takeaways**: This project further strengthened my curtain wall detailing skills and deepened my understanding of BIM-based modeling workflows
