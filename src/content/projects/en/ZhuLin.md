### 🧭 Project Background

This project focuses on the BIM parametric design of the aluminum panel perforation pattern for the **Bamboo Forest Commercial Center** facade. It aims to efficiently translate parametric concepts into construction through digital tools. The workflow primarily uses **Rhinoceros + Grasshopper** for perforation pattern modeling and exports aluminum panel fabrication drawings to guide construction, significantly improving efficiency, precision, and reducing costs.

---

### 🧱 Project Information

- **Location**: Central district of a city
- **Function**: Commercial space
- **Design Goal**: Create visually creative building facades while achieving efficient BIM parametric design and construction data output

3. **Grasshopper Parametric Design**

- Use **Grasshopper** to generate the perforation pattern of the bamboo forest (based on image point distribution and gradient density)
- Control points and random functions create a "dense-to-sparse" pattern
- Parametric design allows quick generation of different facade variations by adjusting only a few parameters

- After generating perforations, subdivide and number aluminum panel blocks for traceability in fabrication and construction
- Output construction drawings with panel dimensions and perforation information
- Generate quantity tables for cost estimation and project management

### 📊 Project Outcomes

- Successfully realized parametric modeling of the bamboo perforation pattern
- Exported **standardized construction drawings + fabrication data**, greatly improving efficiency
- Ensured consistency between design and construction, avoiding errors from manual drafting
- Saved approximately **60%-70%** of design drafting time

---

### 🔮 Summary & Reflection

- **Strengths**: The combination of Rhino + Grasshopper enables rapid modeling and drawing of complex patterns, greatly improving workflow from design to construction
- **Limitations**: For very large facade areas, Grasshopper computation speed may still be a bottleneck, requiring proper subdivision and algorithm optimization
