### 🧭 Project Background

The Nanyang Airport Hotel Project is located on the west side of the T2 Terminal of Yancheng Nanyang International Airport, in Nanyang Town, Tinghu District, Yancheng City. As an important supporting facility for the airport, the hotel provides integrated services including accommodation, conferences, and dining for travelers and business professionals.

The hotel is positioned as a **modern business-oriented space**, emphasizing functionality and comfort. Its architectural and interior design adopts a clean and restrained language, aiming to present a refined urban gateway image.

Unlike my previous projects that primarily relied on **Rhino + Grasshopper** for parametric modeling and fabrication assistance, this project marked my **first formal use of Revit in a real engineering context**, specifically for curtain wall modeling and interdisciplinary collaboration. Due to the need for close coordination with architectural, structural, and MEP disciplines, Revit was chosen as the primary BIM platform to ensure data consistency and efficient collaboration.

In this project, my main responsibilities included:

- Building the external curtain wall model in Revit based on the structural model
- Reporting and coordinating clash issues
- Delivering modeling outputs for further coordination

Although this was my first time using Revit in a real project, I gained valuable experience in Revit-based modeling workflows, BIM coordination, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

### 🧩 BIM Modeling Workflow

#### 1. **Defining Modeling Scope and Coordination Objectives**

- Based on architectural drawings, the modeling scope was defined to include glass curtain walls, aluminum panel systems, and stone curtain walls.
- The main objectives of this modeling phase were:
  1. Building the curtain wall system in Revit based on CAD design drawings
  2. Coordinating with architectural, structural, and MEP models for clash detection
